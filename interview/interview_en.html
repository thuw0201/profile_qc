👋 Part 1: Introduction & Experience

🗣️ Suggested Answer / Câu trả lời gợi ý:

"Yes. My name is Thu. I have over 3 years of experience as a QA/QC Engineer, heavily focused on ERP management systems, E-wallets, and payment platforms.

Currently, I work at ATOM SOLUTION, where my main focus is on manual testing and API testing for multi-modal payment methods. In addition, I am actively learning database verification and automation tools like Playwright to expand my skillset."
💡 Tips (Mẹo): Đừng kể lể về gia đình hay sở thích. Hãy đi thẳng vào: Số năm kinh nghiệm + Domain hiện tại (Payment/ERP) + Kỹ năng nổi bật (API, SQL, Automation).

🗣️ Suggested Answer / Câu trả lời gợi ý:

"I would say my biggest strengths are my documentation skills and attention to detail. In payment systems, a small mistake can cause financial loss, so I always create strict test cases and checklists to ensure data integrity.

Besides, I am a fast learner and constantly seeking self-improvement. Currently, I am dedicating my free time to learning automation testing with Playwright to prepare for future requirements."
💡 Tips (Mẹo): Gắn điểm mạnh của bạn với yêu cầu của ngành Payment: Sự cẩn thận (attention to detail) và tính an toàn dữ liệu (data integrity).

🗣️ Suggested Answer / Câu trả lời gợi ý:

"I have a passion for solving problems and delivering high-quality products to users. I enjoy the process of breaking things logically to find hidden bugs before they reach the customers.

Also, my background and critical thinking skills perfectly align with the mindset needed for testing."
💡 Tips (Mẹo): Nhấn mạnh sự đam mê (passion) trong việc tìm bug và bảo vệ trải nghiệm người dùng.

⚙️ Part 2: Technical & Domain Knowledge

🗣️ Suggested Answer / Câu trả lời gợi ý:

"Yes, I have strong experience in API testing. I mainly use Postman and Swagger.

For payment gateways, I often simulate requests to create orders and mock callback responses from the bank to verify if our system updates the transaction status correctly. I also check status codes, response times, and data fields."
💡 Tips (Mẹo): Nhắc đến các công cụ cụ thể (Postman, Swagger) và bối cảnh thực tế (mock callback responses) để chứng minh bạn thực sự đã làm.

🗣️ Suggested Answer / Câu trả lời gợi ý:

"I have a fundamental understanding of SQL and am familiar with basic commands like SELECT, JOIN, and GROUP BY.

Since my current role focuses mostly on UI and API testing, I haven't had the chance to apply SQL deeply in a real project yet. However, I am a fast learner, and I am very eager to jump in and practice database verification if my new team needs it."
💡 Tips (Mẹo): Nhà tuyển dụng trân trọng sự trung thực. Hãy thẳng thắn thừa nhận việc chưa có kinh nghiệm thực chiến bằng cách ghi điểm nhờ tinh thần sẵn sàng học hỏi.

🗣️ Suggested Answer / Câu trả lời gợi ý:

"Verification means checking documents, designs, or code to ensure we are building the product right. It is a static process without running the software.

Validation means executing the actual software to ensure it meets the customer's requirements. This answers the question: Are we building the right product?"
💡 Tips (Mẹo): Đây là câu hỏi lý thuyết kinh điển của ISTQB. Hãy nhớ: Verification = Không chạy code (Review doc). Validation = Chạy code (Test UI/API).

🤝 Part 3: Behavioral & Situational (Thử thách & Tình huống)

🗣️ Suggested Answer / Câu trả lời gợi ý:

"First, I don't argue. I double-check the requirements and API documentation to make sure I am right.

Then, I will provide clear evidence like screenshots, video records, or Postman logs. Finally, I organize a quick meeting with the Developer and the Business Analyst (BA) to clarify the expected behavior and reach an agreement."
💡 Tips (Mẹo): Thể hiện sự chuyên nghiệp, không cãi vã. Luôn giải quyết dựa trên tài liệu (Specs) và Bằng chứng (Evidence).

🗣️ Suggested Answer / Câu trả lời gợi ý:

"A big challenge was when we tested with a new bank partner, but their staging environment was unstable and frequently offline. It blocked our progress.

To solve this, I studied their API documentation and created mock servers using Postman. This allowed my team to continue testing our internal callback logic independently without waiting for the bank."
💡 Tips (Mẹo): Dùng cấu trúc STAR (Tình huống -> Mục tiêu -> Hành động -> Kết quả). Hành động tạo Mock Server thể hiện sự chủ động của bạn.

🗣️ Suggested Answer / Câu trả lời gợi ý:

"If it's a critical bug, especially in the payment flow, my priority is to report it immediately to the Project Manager and the Dev team.

I will provide all reproduction steps and logs. We must assess the risk together. If the bug causes financial loss or crashes the system, I will suggest delaying the release. Product quality and safety must always come first."
💡 Tips (Mẹo): Với hệ thống thanh toán, Không được thỏa hiệp với lỗi nghiêm trọng. Thể hiện quan điểm mạnh mẽ: "Product quality and safety must always come first".

🗣️ Suggested Answer / Câu trả lời gợi ý:

"In the next two to three years, I want to continue developing my expertise as a QA Engineer specializing in Payment systems.

While manual testing and product flow analysis are my core strengths, my immediate goal is to successfully transition my current Playwright learning into real automation projects. I also hope to take on more responsibilities, like training freshers."
💡 Tips (Mẹo): Cho thấy định hướng rõ ràng và gắn với nội dung công việc JD yêu cầu: phát triển Automation nhưng không bỏ quên Manual/Nghiệp vụ.
